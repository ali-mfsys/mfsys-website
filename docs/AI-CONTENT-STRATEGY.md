# MFSYS AI Content Intelligence

The Content Copilot assists editors; it does not autonomously publish.

## Strategy
1. Identify topic and search intent.
2. Recommend primary and secondary terms.
3. Generate an editorial structure.
4. Recommend metadata and structured data.
5. Improve answerability for AI-powered search.
6. Suggest internal links to MFSYS solutions, products and insights.
7. Require human review and approval before publication.

## AI-search/GEO principles
- Put concise answers near the beginning of relevant sections.
- Use descriptive headings and FAQ structures where useful.
- Prefer factual, attributable claims over generic marketing language.
- Maintain consistent organization, product and industry terminology.
- Use structured data where it accurately represents visible content.
- Keep content authoritative, original and useful; do not generate large volumes of low-value pages.

## Governance
AI-generated suggestions must be reviewed by an authorized editor. Publication remains subject to the existing Draft -> Review -> Approved -> Published workflow.
