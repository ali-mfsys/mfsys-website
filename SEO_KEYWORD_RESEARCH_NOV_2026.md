# MFSYS High-Value Keyword Research & Content Map
**Research snapshot:** November 18–19, 2026  
**Primary public website:** https://mfsys.net  
**Data source:** OpenSEO keyword metrics and sampled live Google results. U.S., Canada, Pakistan, United Arab Emirates; English-language queries.  
**Objective:** Reach qualified institutional buyers, while attracting genuine informational audiences through useful guides.

## Reading the numbers
- Search volume is a provider estimate of monthly searches in a specified country. It is not unique visitors, clicks or MFSYS traffic.
- Keyword Difficulty (KD) is a third-party provider score, not a Google ranking signal.
- Exact-word variants may belong to a common search bucket; **do not add their volumes together** to project traffic.
- The result for **"core banking solution"** was unusually high (**550,000 U.S. monthly searches; KD 92**) relative to "core banking" (4,400), "core banking system" (320) and "core banking software" (210), and identified as navigational. Treat this as an outlier that requires independent verification and Google Search Console corroboration; it is not the basis of this strategy.
- The broad, high-volume phrases "digital banking" and "mobile banking" commonly surface consumer banking results, so product pages should target institutional intent rather than imply a consumer bank.

## Further optimisation priorities
1. Verify **mfsys.net** and **mfsys.ca** in Google Search Console. The legacy domain has significantly more referring domains and may attract older traffic; map URLs before any migration.
2. Gather real query/page impressions and prioritize title and content changes based on actual search performance rather than using estimates alone.
3. Publish client-approved case studies with scope, project dates, named reviewers, deployment stage and verifiable results. Do not invent client testimonials.
4. Ensure release candidates pass typecheck, tests, production build and live route checks before reporting deployment.
5. Track rankings across multiple relevant markets with approved budgets; maintain a difference between procurement intent and broad consumer education.
6. Review query intent and search-volume data periodically rather than making unbounded ranking claims.

**External principles:** Google Search Central recommends people-first content, avoiding doorway pages, keyword stuffing and scaled low-value content. High search volume alone is not proof of business value or ranking potential.
